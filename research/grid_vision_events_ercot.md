## Coverage honesty

- STRONGEST ANCHOR: ERCOT's own government-relations fact sheet
  "Energy Conservation" (January 2024) enumerates every conservation
  request 2008 → Jan 16, 2024. Its 2019–Jan 2024 list matches the
  press-release archive exactly, with ONE discrepancy: the May 13–15
  2022 request (issued as a CEO statement) is in the press archive but
  absent from the fact-sheet count — included as confirmed (ERCOT's
  release is unambiguous) with the classification inconsistency flagged.
- CONFIDENT COMPLETE: conservation appeals + EEAs for 2019, 2020 (zero
  events), 2021, 2022, 2023, Jan 2024 — double-sourced. EEA events are
  complete for the whole window: EEA-3 only during Uri (2021-02-15→19);
  EEA-2 only 2023-09-06; EEA-1 only 2019-08-13 and 2019-08-15 (plus
  transitional EEA-1/2 minutes early on 2021-02-15).
- CONFIDENT ON ABSENCE, Feb 2024–Dec 2025: no conservation appeals or
  EEAs after 2024-01-16 through end-2025 (press archive + independent
  corroboration for summers 2024 and 2025).
- KNOWN GAP: Weather Watch enumeration after TXANS launched (Sept 2023)
  is incomplete — TXANS notices are not always mirrored as press
  releases (the ~Aug 15–18 2024 watch is the known example). Watches
  are context rows anyway, not scored events. Pre-2023 there was no
  "Weather Watch" product; Feb 2022 (Landon) and Dec 2022 (Elliott)
  entries are operations-level Watches.
- WINTER STORM ELLIOTT RESOLVED: ERCOT's post-event report confirms NO
  EEA (PRC min 4,052 MW vs 2,300 MW EEA-1 trigger) and NO public
  conservation appeal in its full communications timeline — an earlier
  automated summary claiming a Dec 23 appeal was disproven by direct
  text extraction of the report.
- TOTALS (event-days): EEA-3: 3 load-shed days (2021-02-15/16/17) + 2
  EEA-3-in-effect days (02-18, 02-19 until 09:00). EEA-2: 1
  (2023-09-06). EEA-1: 2 (2019-08-13, 2019-08-15). Conservation days:
  2019: 5 · 2021: 11 · 2022: 5 (2 fact-sheet-official + 3 May days) ·
  2023: 11 · 2024: 2 · 2025: 0 — total 34.

## Gate-2 v3 scoring sets derived from this list (fixed here, before computation)

- TIER-E days (out-of-sample window 2023–2025): **2023-09-06** — one
  EEA day only. IMPLICATION, stated honestly before computing: with a
  single out-of-sample TIER-E event, the v3 recall floor ("≥ half of
  TIER-E days") degenerates to hit-or-miss on ONE day. The pass bar
  therefore leans on TIER-C days (13 in 2023–2025: Jun 20 + Aug
  17/20/24/25/26/27/29/30 + Sep 6/7 2023, Jan 15/16 2024) for the lift
  statistic, with the 2023-09-06 EEA-2 day REQUIRED to be detected
  (miss = automatic FAIL). This is a pre-registered tightening forced
  by the ground truth's shape, not a post-hoc choice.
- TIER-E days (design-contaminated 2019–2022 window, report-only):
  2019-08-13, 2019-08-15, 2021-02-15→19.
