WHY THE CURTAIN NEVER FAILS HERE

  • Curtain = one vertical strip; its bottom edge is re-sampled against the terrain heightfield every ~120 m and pushed 40 m below ground, so it drapes over ridges with no gaps.
  • side: THREE.DoubleSide — the strip is visible from both faces, so it can’t be back-face-culled at extreme tilt.
  • transparent: true; depthWrite: false; depthTest: true + renderOrder after terrain — kills the z-fighting / vanishing at grazing angles.
  • Ground trace = same polyline draped at terrain + 16 m, so the line itself follows topography.
  • Camera is a damped orbit rig (heading / pitch / distance / target). Pitch clamps 2°–88°; every motion is also on hold-buttons + compass dial — no mouse-drag required.
