   we record ourselves (positions, port dwell, filings-derived events,
   detections) and (b) statistics and estimates we derive from them.
   Estimates are labeled as estimates; confidence scores and evidence
   accompany inferences. Nothing in the service is investment advice.
2. DATA AS-IS. All data is provided as-is, without warranty of
   accuracy, completeness, or availability. Coverage limits stated in
   endpoint metadata (e.g., terrestrial AIS gaps, ADS-B coverage) are
   part of the product description.
3. NO SAFETY-OF-LIFE USE. The service must not be used for navigation,
   aviation/maritime safety, emergency response, or any
   safety-of-life purpose. Fire detections carry NASA's
   not-for-safety-of-life disclaimer downstream.
4. ATTRIBUTION PASSTHROUGH. Responses include license marks per
   endpoint (datacore/LICENSING_AUDIT.md is the register). Customers
   must preserve required attributions when redistributing, including
   ODbL share-alike terms on aircraft-derived databases and Copernicus
   attribution on imagery-derived readings.
5. RATE LIMITS AND FAIR USE. Per-key limits per the published tier
   (requests/minute and /day). Circumvention (key sharing, rotation,
   scraping around limits) is grounds for revocation.
6. KEYS. One key per customer; keys are bearer credentials — customers
   are responsible for keeping them secret; rotation on request.
7. PROHIBITED USES. Reselling raw passthrough of third-party feeds
   where their licenses forbid it (the register governs); representing
   estimates as ground truth; use in violation of applicable law or
   sanctions.
8. TERMINATION. We may suspend keys for terms violations; customers
   may cancel any time; no data deletion obligations apply to already
   published derived statistics.
9. CHANGES. Terms may change with notice to the account email;
   continued use is acceptance.
