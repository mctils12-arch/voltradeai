## The verdict

The system is not properly set up to handle market regimes, volatility or breadth, and the damage is to compounding rather than to risk. Its one real classifier is a VXX-ratio-plus-50-day heuristic whose thresholds were set by in-sample sweeps on survivor universes, with no dwell, no band and a cap table that allows zero stock positions in the calm NEUTRAL state but six in the more stressed CAUTION state; replayed on nineteen years of index data, the exposure schedule that classifier drives compounds at 4.4% a year against 11.1% for buy-and-hold, flips about 28 times a year and sits fully out of the market 39% of the time, while a plain monthly 10-month-average rule reaches 7.9% at a shallower drawdown with 1.6 flips a year. Around that classifier sit four other regime vocabularies that nobody owns — a Markov label fed fractional returns against percent thresholds (so it is pinned to NEUTRAL_BULL on 88% of shadow records yet still shapes exit geometry and ML training), a VIX-level sizing scalar that is dead on the options path, a stress multiplier that clamps to the floor, and a tier size ladder that is computed and then dropped before contract selection — so exposure is cut three times in stress and never by the one quantity the evidence supports, realized volatility. Stock selection does the opposite of what the research supports: it chases the day's largest absolute moves, pays mean-reversion and "buy the dip" bonuses with no regard to the index trend or the stock's own 200-day and 52-week-high position, and has no relative-strength or panic-state guard, which is how it buys falling knives in narrow tapes. The options sleeve has no volatility-state input at all: fixed −0.30 delta, at most two contracts, no crisis pause, no implied-versus-realized ranking, and mark-based 2× stops that turn implied-volatility spikes into realized losses (310 closes inside a day cost −$9.7k of the sleeve's −$9.2k). Breadth is absent. The fix the evidence supports is small, not large: one slow index trend leg plus one loose realized-volatility dial, never fully out of the index, with churn control, a panic-state and 52-week-high guard on stock selection, and volatility-state sizing for short puts — built as a single regime module that every consumer reads, measured forward against the frozen baseline, with every duplicate retired as its consumer flag flips. Doing nothing is not acceptable because the current machinery is measurably the worst arm tested; doing more machinery — ML classifiers, VIX-level exits, extra re-entry delays, a fourth drawdown peak — is wrong on the same evidence, and this plan deliberately builds none of it.

## Decisions, and where this plan departs from the prompt's Sections 6–9 (each with its reason)

1. **Volatility state drives equity de-risking through a continuous realized-vol dial on the gating index, not VIX-level buckets.** VIX level and percentile do not lead drawdowns and forward returns after high readings are higher on average (RESEARCH §3.3, §3.6); the dial is the one element with strong cross-sample evidence (§3.1) and it survives ±20% (§5.4). VIX enters only the short-put sizing path.
2. **The index/floor sleeve is never fully out; only new discretionary risk can go to zero.** Floor 0.5 on the trend legs keeps 70–80% of buy-and-hold CAGR at a drawdown inside the owner's stated tolerance; fully-out keeps 40–65% (§5.3, §5.5). The engine therefore publishes two multipliers: `exposure_multiplier` ∈ {1, .75, .5, .25, 0} for new stock entries and new short-put notional, and `core_multiplier` ∈ [0.5, 1] for the index floor sleeve. The floor's own schedule is **not** changed by this program (see "handed off" below).
3. **Confirmation is a 6-day majority filter on daily votes, not "N consecutive days", and there is no extra re-entry delay.** Bulla's filter is the only confirmation mechanism with honest OOS improvement; extra re-entry delays have no supportive test and cost return (§4.3a, §4.3c). Downgrades take effect as soon as the filter clears; upgrades glide +0.25 per five sessions (tranching, no calendar-day luck); our own ablation found monthly-only upgrades cost 0.5–1 pp of CAGR (§5.4).
4. **Breadth is a caution flag and a selection guard, never an exposure throttle or sell trigger.** Divergence studies are negative or sign-flipped after 2010; only thrusts are consistent, and only as re-entry accelerators (§2.3).
5. **Dip-buys are allowed only when the gating index is above its 200-day, the composite is CALM_BULL (or CHOPPY with healthy breadth), and the market is not in the Daniel–Moskowitz panic state; candidates must sit at ≥75% of their 252-day high and not at a new 20-day low.** Connors' re-test shows the 200-day filter, not the dip, avoided the bears; George–Hwang shows nearness to the high beats discount buying; panic-state losers are option-like, not value (§2.2, §2.4).
6. **Short puts: VIX decile and VVIX size the book down, `VIX/VIX3M > 1` halves new notional, negative VRP blocks new short vol, CRISIS blocks new short puts, ELEVATED moves the target delta from −0.30 toward −0.15 and halves contracts; spreads are not built.** There is no verified evidence for spreads vs naked puts, nor for the 50%/21-DTE/stop rules (§3.6); the sleeve's own stop rule is already under a pre-registered simulation (`research/csp_manager_rule_sim_2026-10-26.md`).
7. **No ML, HMM or jump-model switch.** Honest holdouts show no edge over a vol-targeted twin (§4.2). The jump model is recorded as the benchmark should anyone propose a classifier later.
8. **Universe-agnostic by construction:** the engine takes a gating index; the small/mid sleeve gates on IWM (same functional form, bands scaled to the index's own σ) and carries a monthly Fed-EBP cap (§2.5, §3.5). Cap buckets are reported from the existing Polygon/Finnhub `market_cap` field (`etf_data_sources.fetch_holding_metadata`) for held and selected names, with "unknown" where the field is missing — the scan universe itself has no cap field (AUDIT §4).
9. **Backtest: a regime-layer tool only.** `scripts/regime_backtest.py` reproduces RESEARCH §5 from the repo's bars and is labelled as index-level; it is never presented as a strategy backtest. Validation of the sleeves is forward paper measurement against `BASELINE.md`.

### Step 2 — `regime_engine.py`: the single owner of regime state (flag `VOLTRADE_REGIME_ENGINE`, shadow compute)
- **Files:** `regime_engine.py` (new, pure functions + a thin I/O layer), `test_regime_engine.py` (new), `system_config.py` (one `REGIME_ENGINE_ENABLED` mirror comment + `REGIME_ENGINE_PARAMS` dict), `voltrade_daemon.py` (one method-table line: `"regime_state": ("regime_engine", "regime_state_rpc")`), `storage_config.py` (two paths).
- **Logic (`RegimeState`):** `trend_state` UP/NEUTRAL/DOWN from `trend_score = 0.5·f_slow + 0.25·f_22 + 0.25·f_65` where `f_slow` is the share of positive votes across 120/160/200/240-day lookbacks with a ±1% band and `f_22`, `f_65` are the fast-leg signs (UP ≥ 0.75, DOWN ≤ 0.25); `vol_state` LOW/NORMAL/ELEVATED/CRISIS from the EWMA realized vol (20-day half-life) of the gating index against its own expanding history (LOW < 20th pct, ELEVATED ≥ 80th, CRISIS = ELEVATED and (`VIX/VIX3M > 1` or ≥ 95th pct)); `vol_dial = min(1, σ_target/σ̂)` with σ_target = the index's long-run realized vol fixed ex ante (≥ 2 years, ≤ 10 years of prior data); `breadth_state` market = share of a fixed basket (11 SPDRs + IWM + MDY) above its 200-day (HEALTHY ≥ 0.6, NARROW 0.4–0.6, WEAK < 0.4) and universe = share of the scan's deep-scored names above their own 200-day when the scan supplies it, else `null`; `panic_state` = gating index 24-month return < 0 and 126-day variance in its top quintile; `composite_regime` ∈ {CALM_BULL, VOLATILE_BULL, CHOPPY, STRESSED, BEAR, CRISIS} by the documented decode table; `legacy_label` ∈ {BULL, NEUTRAL, CAUTION, BEAR, PANIC} for consumers that only know the old vocabulary; `exposure_multiplier` = quarter-quantized `(0.5·f_slow + 0.25·f_fast + 0.25·breadth_bin) × vol_dial` for new risk; `core_multiplier = max(0.5, exposure_multiplier)`; `allowed_strategies` (momentum_long, dip_buy, csp, csp_reduced, none) by the table; `confidence` = share of votes agreeing with the composite × data-quality factor (fallback inputs reduce it); `days_in_regime`; `pending_transition` (candidate state, votes in the 6-day window, sessions until it can take effect); `flips_12m`; `inputs` (every reading with its date and source); `stress_flags` (`vix_vix3m_inverted`, `vrp_negative`, `vix_pct_1y`, `vvix`); `ebp` (latest Fed value and date, monthly) for the small/mid sleeve cap.

- **Validation:** Step 1 evaluates the engine's exposure path on SPY and IWM 2007–2026; it must land inside the never-fully-out band of RESEARCH §5.3 (SPY CAGR ≥ 7.5%, max DD ≤ 30%, primary flips ≤ 2/yr); the 2026-10-26 state must match `today_readings` (SPY above 200-day, IWM below 50-day, VIX/VIX3M 0.92, VRP +8.8) — i.e. CALM_BULL on SPY with NARROW breadth and CHOPPY-to-BEAR on IWM.

## What this plan will not do, said plainly
- It will not make the account beat SPY by itself; the evidence says a regime layer buys a shallower drawdown path, not alpha. The stock and options sleeves' returns come from selection and from stopping the hold-time whipsaw, which this plan guards but does not redesign.
- It will not run a strategy-level backtest with delisted names and realistic small-cap fills, because the data to do it honestly does not exist in the repo.
- It will not touch the floor schedule, the halt levels, the kill switches, order transmission, or the exit model.
- It will not add a classifier, a VIX-level exit, a re-entry delay, or a fourth drawdown peak.
